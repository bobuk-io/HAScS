# HAScS 0.3.4

- The scene panel hint in Ukrainian and Russian now says "press a number"
  with the word «номер»: «Виберіть сцену або натисніть номер» and
  «Выберите сцену или нажмите номер». The English hint is unchanged.
- Nothing else changes: scene tiles, keyboard shortcuts, reordering and scene
  activation work as in 0.3.3.

Your Home Assistant connection, Keychain token, scene assignments and settings
are retained. Download the universal ZIP from GitHub Releases, quit HAScS and
replace the app in /Applications. This release does not add an automatic updater.
